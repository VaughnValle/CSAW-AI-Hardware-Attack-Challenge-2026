# Final Challenge
The final challenge for the CSAW AI Hardware Attack Challenge will actually consist of three challenges, each detailed below.

## Timeline
These challenges will take place over 24 hours while at CSAW. The details on the challenges will be released at 12PM EST (subject to change based on scheduling) on 12 November and must be submitted by 12PM EST (subject to change) on 13 November.


## Challenge 1 - Qualifying Trojan in Hardware
The first challenge at CSAW is to implement your Trojan from the qualifying round for the Hackster board itself and demonstrate your Trojan in hardware.

### Scoring
The scoring for this challenge will be more simple than the others. Exact details of the rubrics are still being finalized and will be uploaded at the start of the challenges at CSAW.

## Judging
#### Submission
Each challenge will have its own submission guidelines detailing what artifacts need to be submitted. They will remain similar to those in the qualifying challenge, including documentation and AI logs.

#### Presentation
Each team must present their solutions to the challenges to our panel of judges. This presentation must detail how they used generative AI, specific strategies for each problem, and any challenges faced along the way.

Details on the length of the presentation and questioning sections will be updated once CSAW scheduling is finalized.
